Last updated: November 1, 2026

I’m Giorgi Beria. This policy covers my personal blog at [giorgiberia.github.io](https://giorgiberia.github.io/), where I share projects, services, and thoughts about software development. Other websites and apps linked here have their own privacy practices.

## Hosting

This blog is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. See [GitHub’s Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for how GitHub handles this information.

## Google Analytics and cookies

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and how people use the blog. The Google tag loads automatically; the site currently has no cookie consent banner or on-site analytics opt-out.

Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish visitors and sessions. It collects information such as pages viewed, referring websites, interactions, browser and device details, and approximate location. Your browser sends information, including your IP address, to Google when the tag loads. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses.

Read [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google Analytics’ data safeguards](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245) for more information.

## Embedded content and external resources

Some posts load content directly from other services, including an embedded LinkedIn post and a badge hosted by Shields.io. When you open those posts, your browser connects to those services, which receive your IP address and request information such as browser details and, depending on browser settings, referrer information. Embedded LinkedIn content may also use cookies and associate activity with your LinkedIn account if you are signed in. See [LinkedIn’s Privacy Policy](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy).

Following external links, including links to my main website, project websites, social profiles, and app stores, takes you to services with their own privacy policies.

## Contacting me

There are no account registrations, newsletter signups, checkout forms, or contact forms on this blog. If you contact me by email or through a linked messaging service, I receive the contact details and message content you choose to share so I can respond and discuss your enquiry. The email or messaging provider also processes that communication under its own policies.

## Your choices and questions

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies alone may not prevent requests to Google or embedded-content providers. You can also use a content blocker or [Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) in a supported browser.

For privacy questions or requests concerning messages you have sent me, email [beria.giorgi1@gmail.com](mailto:beria.giorgi1@gmail.com). GitHub, Google, and other service providers control their own retention practices; consult their policies for details.
